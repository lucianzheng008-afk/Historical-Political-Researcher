# Method-Evidence Map

Use this map to connect a design judgment to verified literature without turning a citation into proof. `Method authorities` may contain records cataloged as `method` or `field_foundation` when their content directly supports the operational rule; `debate` and `critique` records belong under disagreement or boundary. Read every record's role, verification status, access status, and limitation before use. Metadata supports metadata claims, abstracts support only their stated claims, and exact page claims require inspection of the cited edition and page.

## PUZ-EXPECTATION — Supported expectation before anomaly

**Operational rule:** State the prior expectation, its source and scope, the conflicting observation, and what resolving the conflict changes theoretically; importance or an alleged gap alone is not a puzzle.

**Method authorities:** METH-CHA-001 (role=field_foundation; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=needs_institution).

**Disagreement or boundary:** The authority supports the rule only at edited-volume level; chapter-specific reasoning requires inspection, and a surprising observation may still reflect measurement error or weak prior literature.

**Required artifact:** Puzzle register with expectation source, observation status, tension, rival interpretations, discriminating evidence, and novelty-verification task.

**Relevant exemplars:** EX-COMP-002 (role=exemplar; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text) illustrates a puzzle-led move; transfer the move, not its conclusion.

## TEMP-SEQUENCE — Order, timing, and duration as causal information

**Operational rule:** Specify the relevant order, timing, duration, and temporal boundary, then show why changing them would change the expected outcome.

**Method authorities:** CN-METH-002 (role=method; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text) and METH-TEMP-002 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution).

**Disagreement or boundary:** Narrative order is not causal sequence unless tied to an explicit temporal model and rivals; abstract-only authority cannot support page-level formulations.

**Required artifact:** Periodization and timeline linking events to rival-specific temporal predictions, lags, duration, and boundary tests.

**Relevant exemplars:** CN-EX-003 (role=exemplar; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text) and EX-COMP-003 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=needs_institution) offer candidate sequencing moves with different access ceilings.

## TEMP-PATH — Path dependence and reproduction

**Operational rule:** Persistence alone is insufficient. Identify the initial choice set, selection event, reproduction or reactive sequence, and durable narrowing of feasible alternatives.

**Method authorities:** METH-TEMP-001 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=open_abstract) and METH-TEMP-004 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution).

**Disagreement or boundary:** Increasing returns is one mechanism, not the definition of all path dependence; reactive sequences, power, conversion, or adaptation may generate different dynamics.

**Required artifact:** Periodization, continuity register, theory-rival matrix, and mechanism-evidence matrix showing choice set, selection, reproduction, interruption, transformation, and rivals.

**Relevant exemplars:** CN-EX-003 (role=exemplar; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text) and EX-COMP-004 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=metadata_only) are limited models for organizing longitudinal analysis.

## TEMP-CRITICAL-JUNCTURE — Critical juncture identification

**Operational rule:** Establish a bounded period of relaxed constraints, feasible alternatives, consequential selection, and post-selection reproduction; a major event or turning-point label is insufficient.

**Method authorities:** METH-TEMP-004 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution).

**Disagreement or boundary:** Accounts differ over whether change must be rapid, agency unusually open, or alternatives counterfactually viable; abstract access supports only the stated claim.

**Required artifact:** Critical-juncture memo with boundaries, alternatives, selecting actors, counterfactuals, immediate consequences, reproduction, and rival breakpoints.

**Relevant exemplars:** EX-COMP-004 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=metadata_only) and EX-COMP-006 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=needs_institution) can orient comparison but not establish a particular juncture.

## INST-GRADUAL-CHANGE — Gradual institutional change

**Operational rule:** Distinguish formal stability from changes in rules, enforcement, interpretation, coalitions, or effects, and identify carriers and mechanisms producing accumulation.

**Method authorities:** CN-METH-002 (role=method; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text).

**Disagreement or boundary:** Slow change is not automatically endogenous institutional change, and stable labels may conceal replacement; broad historical-turn agendas do not prove a particular gradual-change mechanism.

**Required artifact:** Periodization and continuity register coding formal rule, implementation, coalition, function, carrier, interruption, and rival account for each period.

**Relevant exemplars:** CN-EX-001 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=metadata_only) and CN-EX-003 (role=exemplar; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text) have unequal claim ceilings.

## MECH-CHAIN — Entities, activities, and state changes

**Operational rule:** Decompose the explanation into named entities performing activities that transmit change between observable states, with fingerprints and rival predictions for every necessary step.

**Method authorities:** METH-MECH-001 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution) and METH-PT-001 (role=method; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text).

**Disagreement or boundary:** Context is not a residual label, and evidence-test labels do not replace an evidence-generating model; narrative coherence alone is not mechanism evidence.

**Required artifact:** Mechanism-evidence matrix mapping each entity, activity, state change, fingerprint, disconfirming observation, evidence ID, and rival implication.

**Relevant exemplars:** CN-EX-003 (role=exemplar; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text) and EX-IR-001 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=open_full_text) show distinct mechanism-oriented moves.

## EVID-BAYES — Diagnostic evidence and relative likelihood

**Operational rule:** Evaluate evidence by how likely it is under the focal explanation relative to specified rivals, including the process that would create, preserve, and reveal it.

**Method authorities:** METH-PT-001 (role=method; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text), METH-PT-002 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution), and METH-PT-003 (role=method; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text).

**Disagreement or boundary:** Evidence-test labels are qualitative summaries, not fixed strengths. Numerical priors or likelihoods without transparent justification create false precision.

**Required artifact:** Evidence register recording hypotheses, evidence-generation logic, relative likelihood, source dependence, sensitivity, and posterior claim state.

**Relevant exemplars:** None in the current catalog is designated for this rule; do not infer Bayesian practice from an exemplar's topic or prestige.

## CASE-UNIVERSE — Universe before selection

**Operational rule:** Define units, level, time and space bounds, inclusion and exclusion rules, outcome threshold, observation date, and accessible positive, negative, and ambiguous cases before choosing focal cases.

**Method authorities:** METH-CASE-001 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution) and METH-CASE-003 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution).

**Disagreement or boundary:** Case roles depend on the inferential goal and population; a single case may support within-case inference without supporting prevalence or average effects.

**Required artifact:** Case-universe table and selection memo documenting exclusions, missing cases, access asymmetry, strategy, and conclusion ceiling.

**Relevant exemplars:** EX-COMP-002 (role=exemplar; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text) and EX-COMP-007 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=needs_institution) provide comparative scopes but different access ceilings.

## CASE-CONTROLLED-COMPARISON — Controlled historical comparison

**Operational rule:** Ask common focused questions across cases, justify relevant similarities or differences, trace within-case processes, and retain heterogeneity, dependence, and source survival as threats.

**Method authorities:** METH-CASE-001 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution), METH-CASE-003 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution), and CN-METH-001 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=metadata_only).

**Disagreement or boundary:** CN-DEB-002 (role=critique; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text) and METH-CASE-002 (role=debate; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution) challenge overconfidence without invalidating every controlled comparison.

**Required artifact:** Structured-focused protocol, common-question matrix, within-case tests, dependence audit, negative or shadow case, and bounded inference statement.

**Relevant exemplars:** EX-COMP-003 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=needs_institution) and EX-COMP-006 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=needs_institution) require full-text reconstruction before use.

## CONCEPT-TRAVEL — Concepts across historical settings

**Operational rule:** Reconstruct attributes, boundaries, original cases, actor meanings, and hidden assumptions, then test membership and measurement before retaining, differentiating, or revising a concept.

**Method authorities:** METH-CONCEPT-001 (role=field_foundation; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution).

**Disagreement or boundary:** METH-CONCEPT-002 (role=debate; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=open_full_text) disputes how membership should adapt, while CN-FIELD-002 (role=field_foundation; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=metadata_only) supplies a locality claim rather than a completed travel test. METH-AGENDA-001 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution) is agenda-setting and cannot serve as the sole authority for concept travel.

**Required artifact:** Concept register with intension, extension, actor-language translation, membership rules, negative cases, indicators, variants, and revision decision.

**Relevant exemplars:** CN-EX-002 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=open_abstract) and EX-COMP-002 (role=exemplar; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text) offer concept-use moves with unequal evidence access.

## MEASURE-VALIDITY — Concept-to-indicator validity

**Operational rule:** Link concept to dimensions, indicators, coding rules, and source opportunities, then test reliability, equivalence, and outcome contamination.

**Method authorities:** METH-MEASURE-001 (role=field_foundation; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=open_full_text) and METH-SOURCE-002 (role=method; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text).

**Disagreement or boundary:** METH-CONCEPT-002 (role=debate; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=open_full_text) shows that explicit membership rules remain necessary; transparent coding alone cannot cure construct invalidity.

**Required artifact:** Measurement memo and concept register mapping dimensions to indicators, source families, coding decisions, ambiguity, validation checks, and disconfirming cases.

**Relevant exemplars:** EX-COMP-002 (role=exemplar; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text) and EX-COMP-006 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=needs_institution) invite different levels of measurement audit.

## SOURCE-HISTORIOGRAPHY — Source selection and historiographic position

**Operational rule:** Explain how the corpus was produced and selected, distinguish interpretations from records, and audit provenance, genre, incentives, dependence, survival, and researcher selection.

**Method authorities:** METH-SOURCE-002 (role=method; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text).

**Disagreement or boundary:** METH-SOURCE-001 (role=critique; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution) and METH-SOURCE-003 (role=critique; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=open_abstract) are screening critiques, not substitutes for source criticism or historiographic training. METH-AGENDA-001 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution) covers the historical turn broadly and is not a source-criticism authority.

**Required artifact:** Source register, source-dependence matrix, corpus protocol, historiography memo, and claim-level separation of observation, interpretation, and inference.

**Relevant exemplars:** EX-IR-001 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=open_full_text) and EX-COMP-004 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=metadata_only) expose different source-access problems.

## SOURCE-SILENCE — Absence and record-generating processes

**Operational rule:** Treat absence as evidence only after showing that the record was likely to be created, recorded honestly, preserved, catalogued, accessible, and found; otherwise code it as not observed.

**Method authorities:** None in the current catalog; do not recode a critique as a method authority.

**Disagreement or boundary:** METH-SOURCE-001 (role=critique; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution) supports a source-selection audit but supplies no universal probability threshold for silence.

**Required artifact:** Silence audit with every generation-and-discovery link, uncertainty, at least two alternative source routes, and findings that change diagnostic value.

**Relevant exemplars:** None in the current catalog is designated for this rule; do not infer a silence protocol from publisher metadata.

## THEORY-LOCALITY — Local origins and transported scope

**Operational rule:** Recover where a theory arose, its original objects and assumptions, then test what travels and judge retention, bounding, differentiation, revision, or rejection by explanatory performance.

**Method authorities:** CN-FIELD-002 (role=field_foundation; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=metadata_only) and METH-CONCEPT-001 (role=field_foundation; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution).

**Disagreement or boundary:** CN-DEB-001 (role=debate; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=metadata_only) and CN-DEB-003 (role=critique; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=open_abstract) frame competing field positions; historical or foreign origin neither validates nor refutes a proposition.

**Required artifact:** Theory-locality audit recording origin, original cases, hidden conditions, traveled conditions, comparative performance, rivals, and bounded disposition.

**Relevant exemplars:** CN-EX-001 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=metadata_only) and CN-EX-002 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=open_abstract) show local agendas but do not transfer conclusions.

## REVIEW-CEILING — Conclusion bounded by weakest necessary link

**Operational rule:** Cap every conclusion at the weakest necessary step in design, source access, measurement, mechanism evidence, comparison, and verification; report unresolved results rather than filling gaps.

**Method authorities:** METH-PT-001 (role=method; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text), METH-CASE-001 (role=method; verification_status=publisher_abstract; access_status=needs_institution), and METH-SOURCE-002 (role=method; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text).

**Disagreement or boundary:** METH-MEASURE-001 (role=field_foundation; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=open_full_text) supplies a measurement-chain boundary but not a universal evidence hierarchy; method-specific ceilings still apply.

**Required artifact:** Review report with claim-by-claim ceiling, weakest-link diagnosis, access state, rival status, repair, and verification test.

**Relevant exemplars:** EX-IR-001 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=open_full_text), EX-COMP-002 (role=exemplar; verification_status=full_text; access_status=open_full_text), and EX-COMP-005 (role=exemplar; verification_status=publisher_metadata; access_status=needs_institution) display distinct access and scope ceilings.
