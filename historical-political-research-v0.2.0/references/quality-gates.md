# Quality Gates

## Blocking gates

A full-pipeline project does not advance to polished drafting while any applicable blocking gate fails:

1. The task and target output are explicit.
2. A political-science puzzle exists, not only a broad topic or literature gap.
3. The question can be answered with evidence in the available time.
4. Key concepts have non-circular definitions and observable indicators.
5. The outcome and its timing are defined independently of the proposed cause.
6. The historical dimension changes the explanation rather than decorating it.
7. Period boundaries have substantive reasons and are not chosen only after seeing the outcome.
8. A continuity claim identifies carriers, reproduction, interruption, and transformation.
9. A path-dependence claim identifies alternatives, a selection event, reproduction or increasing returns, and lock-in or durable narrowing.
10. The focal mechanism names entities, activities, state changes, and observable fingerprints.
11. At least one serious rival has different observable implications.
12. The case universe and selection logic are recorded before general claims are made.
13. Negative, shadow, or outcome-absent cases are considered.
14. Discovery evidence is not silently reused as independent confirmation.
15. Source dependence, access asymmetry, silence, and missingness are audited.
16. Claims are linked to evidence IDs and assigned honest states.
17. The conclusion stays at or below the design-and-evidence ceiling.

When a gate fails, state the failure, its consequence, and the smallest repair. Do not mask it with fluent prose.

## Conclusion ceilings

| Design/evidence state | Maximum defensible conclusion |
|---|---|
| Broad reading or proposal only | candidate puzzle, plausible mechanism, and research plan |
| Single positive case with dependent sources | bounded interpretation or hypothesis generation |
| Single case with all necessary mechanism steps supported, serious rivals tested, and no weaker source ceiling | mechanism operated in this case, within stated scope |
| Multiple positive cases without negative cases | repeated operation among selected outcomes, not necessity or sufficiency |
| Structured comparison with negative cases and comparable access | bounded comparative causal claim |
| Nested or population evidence plus process tests | population pattern plus case-level mechanism, within model and sampling limits |
| Missing decisive evidence | unresolved, pending, or contested claim |

Source quality can lower but never raise the ceiling set by the design.

## Rapid mode

When time is limited, retain the minimum chain:

1. task contract;
2. one puzzle card and one fallback;
3. question, answer candidate, and contribution ceiling;
4. mechanism plus strongest rival;
5. case universe and selection warning;
6. five priority evidence searches;
7. conclusion ceiling and stop conditions.

Rapid mode may reduce breadth, never evidence honesty or fabrication safeguards.

## Completion checklist

Before calling a project or review complete, verify:

- every central claim has a stable ID and state;
- every cited source exists and its access level is stated;
- quotations, dates, page numbers, DOIs, and archive identifiers were checked;
- the puzzle survives a “so what” and a prior-literature test;
- the title matches the achieved contribution;
- the research plan contains sequence, dependencies, risks, and stop rules;
- case-selection and periodization memos are present where applicable;
- rivals receive a fair test using comparable evidence;
- contradictions and unresolved evidence are visible;
- venue constraints are mechanically checked;
- the final output distinguishes finding, inference, limitation, and next step.

## Stop language

Use explicit statements when evidence is insufficient:

- “This is a candidate explanation, not an established finding.”
- “The available evidence does not distinguish the focal mechanism from the rival.”
- “No record was observed; the search cannot establish that the event did not occur.”
- “The selected cases support within-case inference but not a population-level claim.”
- “The novelty claim remains provisional pending a broader literature search.”
