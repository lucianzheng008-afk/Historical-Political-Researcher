# Exemplar Playbook

Use each entry as a design lesson, not substantive evidence for another case. Claims below stay within catalog metadata, abstract, or full-text access. Verify cited text before adding details, quotations, or page claims.

### Exemplar 01 — Nuclear non-use as a puzzle

**Catalog ID and access:** `EX-IR-001`; `publisher_metadata` / `open_full_text`, but page claims require opening the relevant version.
**Puzzle:** Why did nuclear non-use persist despite material incentives that could predict use?
**Prior expectation and rival:** Material utility is the baseline expectation; a normative mechanism is the catalog-supported rival to test, not an assumed answer.
**Contribution:** Shows how an expectation violation can organize historical IR inquiry.
**Design, cases, and period:** United States nuclear non-use; exact temporal boundaries must be verified in the full text.
**Temporal move:** Sequence the emergence and reproduction of restraint rather than infer a norm from duration alone.
**Mechanism or inferential move:** Trace observable steps connecting normative claims to decisions while testing material rivals.
**Evidence families:** Publisher metadata indicates historical security analysis; identify decision records, public justifications, and historiography only after full-text verification.
**Learnable move:** Turn durable non-use into a rival-based temporal mechanism test.
**Do not transfer:** Do not import the nuclear-taboo conclusion, source interpretation, or periodization into another security case.
**Relevant modes and rules:** Puzzle, analysis, review; `PUZ-EXPECTATION`, `TEMP-SEQUENCE`, `MECH-CHAIN`, `SOURCE-HISTORIOGRAPHY`, `REVIEW-CEILING`.
